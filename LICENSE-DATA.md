The derived data are:

> Mai, Q. H., Nguyen, D. P., Pham, T. T., and Ho, T. L. H. (2026). HEC-18 pier scour against field data: Measured versus maximum scour and bridge-level bounds. Journal of Hydraulic Engineering (submitted). Data and code: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24601656.

## Source data: public domain

The source data are the USGS Pier‑Scour Database (PSDb‑2014; Benedict, S.T., and Caldwell, A.W., 2014, U.S. Geological Survey Data Series 845). They are a work of the U.S. Government and are in the public domain. The CC BY 4.0 licence covers only the cleaning, the quality flags and the analysis results added here, not the original USGS records. Please also cite Benedict & Caldwell (2014) when you use the data.
